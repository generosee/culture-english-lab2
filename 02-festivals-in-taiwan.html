3. Reading

Taiwan has many colourful festivals. Three of the most famous are Lunar New Year, the Lantern Festival, and the Moon Festival.

Lunar New Year is the biggest holiday. Before it starts, families clean their homes and decorate their doors with red paper. Red is the colour of good luck. On New Year's Eve, everyone comes home for a big reunion dinner. Children get red envelopes with money inside. This tradition is very popular with kids!

Fifteen days later, people celebrate the Lantern Festival. Children carry small lanterns, and cities hold big lantern shows. In Pingxi, people write wishes on sky lanterns and let them fly into the night sky.

The Moon Festival comes in autumn, when the moon is full and bright. Families eat mooncakes and pomelos. In Taiwan, many people also have a barbecue with friends and neighbours. Some say this tradition started from a TV advertisement, but now it is part of the holiday.

4. Check Your Understanding

  1. Why do people use red paper during Lunar New Year?
    Show answer

    Because red is the colour of good luck.

  2. What do children get on New Year's Eve?
    Show answer

    Red envelopes with money inside.

  3. What do people do with sky lanterns in Pingxi?
    Show answer

    They write wishes on them and let them fly into the sky.

  4. Name two foods people eat at the Moon Festival.
    Show answer

    Mooncakes and pomelos (and barbecue food).

5. Speak & Act

Festival Tour Guide (groups of 3): Choose one festival. Prepare a 1-minute talk for a foreign visitor. Use at least four vocabulary words.

Useful frames: “This festival is in …” · “People usually …” · “The special food is …” · “My favourite part is …”
